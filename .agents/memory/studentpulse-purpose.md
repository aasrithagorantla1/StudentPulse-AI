---
name: StudentPulse AI product purpose
description: The intended audience and goals of the StudentPulse AI student-success platform.
---

StudentPulse AI is intended for colleges to monitor academic progress, identify students who may need support, improve placement readiness, and track interventions.

**Why:** The user defined these as the product's core purpose.

**How to apply:** Keep future work focused on college student success and these four outcomes.
